## Plain-English summary

This summary is not part of the agreement and is not legally binding. The
numbered sections below are what the Entity is agreeing to.

- **The Entity keeps its copyright.** Nothing is assigned or sold to Macro. The
  Entity can reuse its own contributions however it likes.
- **The Entity grants Macro a broad license**, including the right to relicense
  contributions — which is what lets Macro ship under the AGPLv3 and also sell
  commercial licenses.
- **The Entity confirms it has the rights it is granting**, and lists the people
  authorized to contribute on its behalf in Schedule A.
- **Macro's commitments** to keeping the Project open source, and to reinvesting
  commercial revenue into the community, are in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).
