## Plain-English summary

This summary is not part of the agreement and is not legally binding. The
numbered sections below are what you are agreeing to.

- **You keep your copyright.** You are not selling or assigning your work to
  us. You can reuse your own contribution anywhere, for anything, forever.
- **You give us a broad license to it**, including the right to license it to
  other people under terms we choose. That is what lets us ship Macro under the
  AGPLv3 *and* sell commercial licenses to companies that cannot comply with
  the AGPLv3.
- **You confirm you have the right to contribute it** — that it is your work,
  that you are not pasting in code you do not have the rights to, and that if
  your employer owns what you write, you have their permission.
- **We commit to keeping Macro open source.** Those commitments live in
  [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), including how we reinvest commercial revenue
  into the community.

Why we ask for this at all, and why a license grant rather than a copyright
assignment, is explained in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md#why-we-ask-for-a-cla).
